Our Story

A Creative

Southern Kitchen

At Touch of Flavor, we blend the warmth of home-style cooking with a modern, artistic touch. Our space is more than a restaurant—it's a cultural experience. Surrounded by soulful art celebrating legends of music, marble tables, and plush blue velvet seating, every meal feels like a special occasion.

Our kitchen crafts bold, authentic flavors—from our colossal fried shrimp to our slow-simmered seafood gumbo. We invite you to sit back, enjoy the vibe, and savor a truly creative Southern experience.
